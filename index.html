Her first day, hour by hour

What Kozhikode has been keeping for you

Not a plan so much as a promise. This is the day I have been rehearsing in my head since the date was fixed.

The first hour

Arrivals gate, Karipur കരിപ്പൂർ

I will be there far too early, on the wrong side of the barricade, watching the doors. You will walk out and I will forget every single thing I planned to say.

Late morning

Sulaimani at a roadside thattukada

Down from the airport plateau, first stop on the way. Black tea with lime, too sweet, in a small glass. You will complain about the sugar and finish it anyway.

Midday

Mittai Theruvu, for halwa മിഠായിത്തെരുവ്

Sweet Meat Street, where the Kozhikode halwa sits in slabs of black, orange and green behind the glass. We will taste four and buy far more than two people can carry.

Afternoon

Kozhikode biryani, and then nothing at all

Short-grain kaima rice, fried onions, the whole thing sealed under dough. Afterwards we do absolutely nothing for two hours, which is the actual point of the day.

6:07 pm

Sunset on the old pier, Kozhikode Beach

The sun goes into the Arabian Sea straight ahead of us, off the broken pier. December light, no rain. This is the sunset all the others have been standing in for.

Night

Beypore, where the water goes quiet ബേപ്പൂർ

The uru boatyards, the smell of teak and rope, the Chaliyar meeting the sea. Nobody around. Just the water moving, and you finally here, and no more counting.

Shilpummaa —

I built this because ninety days is a long time to hold something in your chest without giving it somewhere to go. So I gave it a page, and a clock, and a coastline.

People here say the sea is loudest right before it turns calm. That has been the whole year: loud, restless, aimed entirely at one date in December. Everything I do lately quietly arranges itself around 9 December 2026.

You are the love of my life. Not in the way people say it easily — in the plain, stubborn way, the kind that keeps a countdown running on a webpage nobody asked for.

Come soon. Kozhikode is holding a sunset for you, and so am I.

— Yours, always, and counting.Written 10 September 2026 · Kozhikode
